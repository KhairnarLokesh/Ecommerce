2. Typography Hierarchy

Google Fonts pairing: Outfit for authoritative commercial headings, Inter for high-legibility body data and pricing.

Heading 1 – 32px / 2.0rem Bold (Outfit)

Used for main page titles and hero banners

Heading 2 – 26px / 1.625rem Bold (Outfit)

Used for section titles and modal headers

Heading 3 – 20px / 1.25rem Bold (Outfit)

Used for product names and card titles

Body Text – 16px / 1.0rem Regular (Inter). Clear contrast for commercial catalog browsing, invoice item descriptions, and delivery guidelines.

Footnote / Microcopy – 13px / 0.8125rem. Used for GST notes, status timestamps, and legal caveats.
